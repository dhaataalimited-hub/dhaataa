DuoPrompt

Look at the lens, not at your notes.

A teleprompter camera for iPhone. On iPhone Duo, the outer screen becomes the prompter for the rear cameras, so your script sits right beside the lens while you record.

How it works Support

For iPhone, iOS 26 or later. Made for iPhone Duo.

What DuoPrompt is

Your script, right beside the camera

Open your iPhone Duo and turn it around. You face the rear cameras and the outer screen, where your script scrolls right next to the lens, so you can look straight into the camera while you read. The inner screen faces away with the camera controls.

On every other iPhone, and on iPhone Duo when it’s closed, your script appears at the top of the screen next to the front camera. The same scripts, style and recording settings work everywhere.

🎙️

It follows your voice

With Follow Voice, the script moves as you speak, waits when you ad-lib and finds you again if you skip ahead. Or scroll at a steady speed and change it live.

  • Speech recognition runs on your iPhone
  • Works offline once iOS has its speech model
  • Pick voice or steady scrolling for each take
🎥

A proper camera

Only the options your iPhone’s camera supports are shown, so every combination you pick will record.

  • 720p, 1080p or 4K, at up to 60 fps
  • HEVC or H.264, HDR and stabilisation
  • External microphones, with a live level meter
✍️

Scripts and takes

Everything from the first draft to the finished take, in one app.

  • Write, paste, dictate or import scripts (text, Markdown, RTF, PDF)
  • Colours, three fonts, size, bold, line spacing and margins
  • Trim takes and save them to Photos or Files, or share them, without re-encoding

Private by design

Your words stay on your iPhone

DuoPrompt has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server. Your scripts and takes never leave your iPhone unless you share them.

🔒

No data collected

Nothing about you or your recordings is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

📱

On-device speech

Follow Voice and dictation use Apple’s on-device speech recognition.

Questions

Short FAQ

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. DuoPrompt works on any iPhone with iOS 26 or later: the script sits at the top of the screen, next to the front camera. iPhone Duo adds the outer-screen prompter for the rear cameras.

How much does it cost?

DuoPrompt is free to download, and your first take is free. To keep going, choose a yearly plan with a free week, a monthly plan, a one-off Lifetime purchase, or a Day Pass for 24 hours. Prices are shown in the App Store in your currency.

Where are my scripts and takes kept?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Save takes you want to keep to Photos or Files.

When does it launch?

DuoPrompt is with Apple for review soon. Questions in the meantime? Email hello@dhaataa.com or see Support.
