What Righto is

AI governance for managed Macs

Righto is an AI governance tool for small and mid-sized businesses running managed Macs — fleets of up to 500 devices. One package deploys through any Apple MDM and gives IT and compliance teams a clear picture of how AI is actually being used at work.

It discovers which AI tools are in use on company Macs — AI apps, coding agents, local models, MCP servers and browser extensions — coaches employees on safe AI use at the moment it matters instead of simply blocking them, and turns that activity into evidence you can hand to an auditor, a regulator or an insurer.

Righto is installed by an organisation on the Macs it manages. It is distributed only through Apple MDM enrolment by the organisation that owns or manages those devices, and is not offered for use on personal or unmanaged Macs.

Questions

Short FAQ

Who is it for?

Small and mid-sized businesses running managed Macs — typically up to 500 devices — where IT, security or compliance needs to know how AI is being used at work. It's built for the team that administers the fleet, and for whoever has to answer questions about AI from an auditor, a board or an insurer.

Which MDMs work?

Righto ships as a single package that deploys through any Apple MDM, including Jamf, Kandji / Iru, Mosyle, Microsoft Intune and Addigy. If you manage Macs with something else that supports standard Apple MDM deployment, it will work there too.

What data does it collect?

By default Righto collects metadata about AI tool use — which AI apps, coding agents, local models, MCP servers and browser extensions are present and being used on an organisation-managed Mac — not the contents of the files, prompts or code involved.

Righto only runs on Macs an organisation has enrolled through its own MDM, and employees on those Macs can see exactly what it collects. The Righto privacy policy sets out what it collects and what it deliberately does not.

When does it launch?

Righto is in development and we haven't set a public launch date. Join the waitlist above and we'll let you know as it opens up.
